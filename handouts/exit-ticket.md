canvas before anyone leaves.

## What the instructor does with it

| Question | Used for |
|---|---|
| 1 | Reaction. Which blocks to protect when time is short |
| 2 | The labs. An answer that shows up three times becomes a fix in the lab README and a line in [TROUBLESHOOTING.md](../docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md) before the next room |
| 3 | Learning. Compare with the same question in the pre-work |
| 4 | Follow-through. This sentence is quoted back in the day-30 email |
