# Handouts

Everything the instructor sends or prints for the engineers' day. Each one is ready to copy:
change the bracketed parts and send. The handouts for the two-hour leaders' session are in
[leaders/handouts/](../leaders/handouts/README.md).

| Handout | When | Who gets it |
|---|---|---|
| [Pre-work email](pre-work-email.md) | One week before | Every participant |
| [Workflow canvas](workflow-canvas.md) | Printed, one per pair, on the table at 9:00 | Every pair |
| [Exit ticket](exit-ticket.md) | 4:45 on the day | Every participant |
| [Follow-up emails](follow-up-emails.md) | The next morning, week one, day 30, day 60 to 90 | Participants and the sponsor |
| [Five questions for leaders](questions-for-leaders.md) | The close of the [leaders' session](../leaders/README.md) | Leaders and the sponsor |

The canvas is the one that matters most. It is filled in a row at a time during the day, finished
in the last block, and it is what the day-30 email asks about.
