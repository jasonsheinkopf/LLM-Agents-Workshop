# Follow-up emails

Four short messages. They are what turns a good day into something that changes work.

## The next morning: recap

**Subject:** Yesterday's agents workshop: what you built, and what's next

Hi all,

Yesterday every pair built an agent that answers from a handbook, books through a confirmation
step, and is measured by an experiment you can re-run. Your code is in your own clone, and your
traces and experiments are in your Langfuse project. Both are yours to keep.

Three things:

- **Your canvas.** A photo of your pair's workflow canvas is attached. The last row names an owner
  and a next step. That is the part that matters now.
- **One improvement, the right way.** If you have an hour this week, do
  [part 2 of the last lab](../labs/07-ship-it/README.md#after-the-day-one-improvement-the-right-way):
  pick a failing case, read its trace, fix the right layer, re-run the experiment.
- **Office hour.** [Day, time, link.] Bring whatever you are stuck on, in the labs or in your own
  workflow.

1. Did you build it, or something else? What happened?
