## What the facilitator does with it

| Question | Used for |
|---|---|
| 1 | Learning. Compare with the same question in the [pre-session email](pre-session-email.md) |
| 2 | Follow-through. This sentence is quoted back in the [day-30 email](follow-up-emails.md#day-30-the-review). It is the evidence for outcome 2 |
| 3 | Reaction. Which blocks to protect when time is short |
| 4 | The session. An answer that shows up three times becomes a change to a handout or to the [facilitator guide](../FACILITATOR.md) before the next room |
