# Pre-session email

Send one week before. It asks for about 15 minutes. Send it from the sponsor, or with the sponsor
copied: leaders answer their own sponsor. Send a one-line reminder two days before to anyone who
has not replied.

---

**Subject:** Before [day, date]: 15 minutes of preparation for the AI agents session

Hi [name],

On [day, date] this group spends two hours deciding where an AI agent would earn its place in
[organization], and where it would not. It is a working session, not a presentation. There are no
laptops and no code. By the end, the group will have picked one pilot, named its owner, and
written down what the pilot has to prove before anyone trusts it.

To make that possible, two things before the session. Together they take about 15 minutes.

**1. Send two workflows from your part of the organization.** Pick work that happens every week,
where someone has to look something up or decide something. For each one, reply with:

- What starts it? (a request arrives, a customer calls, a report is due)
- What does someone need to know or look up to do it?
- What is the result? (a reply, an approval, a booking, a document)
- Roughly how often does it happen, and how long does one take?

Do not pick the most impressive idea. Pick the work your teams repeat. The group will score these
workflows in the session and choose one.

**2. Answer two questions.**

- From 1 to 5, how confident are you that you could tell whether an AI agent is good enough to
  put in front of customers or staff?
- What is your biggest doubt about using AI agents here? One sentence. The doubts go on the wall
  in the session, without names, and the pilot has to answer them.

**Data.** Describe the workflow, not the people in it. Please send no customer names, no personal
details and nothing confidential.

**The session.** [Time], [room]. Two hours, with one short break. Bring a pen. Laptops stay
closed.

[sponsor]

---

## What the facilitator does with the replies

| Reply | Used for |
|---|---|
| The workflows | Printed one per slip, and shared out so that every table has at least three. They are the material for Block 4 |
| The confidence score | The baseline. The [exit card](exit-card.md) asks the same question |
| The doubts | Written one per card, without names, and put on the wall before the session. In Block 6 each one becomes a test case or a reason to stop |
| No reply | A reminder two days before. Then ask the sponsor for three spare workflows |
