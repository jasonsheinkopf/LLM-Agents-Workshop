# Ridgeline Climbing Gym: Staff & Front-Desk Handbook

> Ridgeline Climbing Gym is a fictional gym made up for this workshop. Every policy, price and
> person in this file is invented. Do not call a real gym and quote it.

## Hours and location

Ridgeline is open Monday to Friday from 6:00 to 22:00, and Saturday and Sunday from 8:00 to 20:00.
The gym is closed on New Year's Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. On other public holidays it
keeps weekend hours. The address is 400 Quarry Lane. Free parking is in the lot behind the building,
and there is a bike rack by the front door.

## Day passes and prices

An adult day pass (18 and over) is $24. A youth day pass (ages 6 to 17) is $18. Children under 6
climb free with a paying adult, in the kids' bouldering area only. A 10-visit punch card is $200 and
never expires. Students and military get $4 off a day pass with valid ID. Rental shoes are $6, and a
harness is $5. A chalk bag is included free with a harness rental.

## Memberships

An adult monthly membership is $85, and a youth monthly membership is $60. A family membership
covers two adults and up to three children for $190 a month. Members can bring a guest at half the
day-pass price. Memberships can be paused for up to three months a year, for a $10 monthly hold fee.
To cancel a membership, give 30 days' notice at the front desk or by email. Memberships cannot be
cancelled over the phone.

## Waivers

Every climber must have a signed waiver on file before climbing or taking a class. Adults sign their
own. Anyone under 18 needs a waiver signed by a parent or legal guardian. Guardians can sign online
before the visit or in person at the front desk. A waiver stays valid for one year. Staff cannot
accept a waiver signed by a sibling, a babysitter or a coach.

## Kids and supervision

Children under 14 must be supervised by an adult (18 or over) who stays inside the gym for the
whole visit. That adult does not need to climb. Children under 12 may boulder and climb on the
auto-belay walls, but they may not belay anyone. Climbers aged 12 to 17 may belay once they pass
the Intro to Belay class, or the belay test.

## Youth programs

**Youth Climbing** is Ridgeline's class for ages 7 to 11. Kids learn movement and safety through
games, bouldering, and top-rope climbing on the auto-belay walls. Staff handle every rope, so
nobody in the class belays. It runs on Saturday and Sunday mornings at 10:00, for 90 minutes, and
costs $35. A parent or guardian must stay on site during the class. Youth Climbing is the stepping
stone to Intro to Belay, which a child can take from age 12.

## Adult and teen classes

**Intro to Belay** is for ages 12 and up. It teaches knots, partner checks and belaying on top rope,
and ends with the belay test. **Lead Climbing 101** is for ages 16 and up, and needs a belay
certification. **Climber's Yoga** is for ages 16 and up. Every class includes rental gear. Classes
can be booked by phone, online or at the front desk. A class booked over the phone needs the
participant's name and age, and a confirmation text is sent to the number on file.

## Belay certification

To belay on the top-rope walls without staff, a climber must pass the belay test. The test checks a
correct figure-eight follow-through knot, a partner check, a correct belay, catching a fall, and
lowering. Climbers who already belay can take the test for free at the front desk, at any time
except Saturday 10:00 to 13:00. Anyone who fails the test must take Intro to Belay before trying
again. A belay certification from another gym is not accepted. Everyone tests at Ridgeline.

## Cancellations and refunds

A class can be cancelled for a full refund up to 24 hours before it starts. A cancellation made less
than 24 hours before the class gets a gym credit instead of a refund. No-shows get no refund or
credit. Day passes and punch cards are not refundable. If Ridgeline cancels a class, everyone gets a
full refund automatically.

## Birthday parties

Birthday parties run on Saturdays and Sundays at 12:00 and 15:00, for two hours. A party is $300 for
up to 10 children, plus $25 for each extra child, up to 16 in total. The price includes gear, a
dedicated instructor and a party room for 45 minutes. Parties must be booked at least two weeks
ahead with a $100 deposit. Phone staff can answer questions about parties, but cannot book them.
Parties are booked by the events coordinator, so transfer the caller to staff.

## Safety and injuries

If a caller reports an injury, a medical emergency, or a safety problem at the gym, do not give
medical advice. For an emergency happening right now, tell them to call 911. For anything else
about an injury or an incident, transfer the caller to the duty manager straight away.

## Lost and found

Lost items are kept at the front desk for 30 days, then donated. Staff can check for an item if the
caller describes it, but they never hold valuables over the phone.

## Accessibility

The main floor, the bouldering area and the restrooms are step-free. Adaptive climbing sessions
run on the first Sunday of each month, from 9:00 to 11:00, with trained volunteers. They are free for
the climber and one companion. Service animals are welcome in the lobby and the viewing area, but
not on the climbing floor.
